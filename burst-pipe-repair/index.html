Pipes that burst rank among the most frequent causes of water damage, and they can do devastating harm to a building. At 4 to 8 gallons a minute, just one burst can send 240 to 480 gallons each hour into your ceilings, walls and flooring. Pipes burst from freezing, old plumbing, too much water pressure, corrosion or a physical hit, and in every case a fast emergency response is what keeps water from ruining flooring, structural materials, building systems and your belongings.

When a pipe bursts in Sterling Heights or the surrounding area, Sterling Heights Restoration runs an emergency response around the clock. We can help find the shutoff and stop the water, and our team works with licensed plumbers to see that the pipe itself is repaired correctly. With the source under control, our crew goes straight to extraction, using truck-mounted machines and portable extractors to clear standing water from each affected area quickly.

Structural drying comes next and covers the wet drywall, the framing, the insulation, the subfloor and the finished flooring above it. Since water runs downhill through ceiling spaces, floor assemblies and wall cavities, a burst pipe frequently affects several rooms on more than one level. Our thermal imaging and moisture detection equipment traces water migration paths so every affected area is identified and properly dried, which prevents the mold growth and structural deterioration that hidden moisture causes.

Burst Pipe Repair Prevention Tips

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • Have a licensed plumber inspect your plumbing system every few years for wear and corrosion
  • Unheated spaces like garages, attics and crawl spaces need pipe insulation on exposed lines
  • During freezing weather, keep faucets dripping and cabinet doors open so heat can circulate
  • If your home still has polybutylene or aging galvanized steel lines, replace them before one gives out
  • Put leak sensors by the water heater and along the main supply line
  • Monitor your water pressure, because excessive pressure (above 80 PSI) strains pipes and fittings

What a Professional Brings to Burst Pipe Repair

What you can see after a pipe bursts is usually only part of the damage. It moves between ceiling joists, inside floor assemblies, beside electrical conduit and down wall cavities, soaking materials you might not find for days or even weeks. Hidden spaces may hold established mold by the time you notice, with structural materials seriously weakened as well. With moisture meters and thermal cameras, trained technicians follow the water's path and pinpoint every affected area right away.

Professional-grade extraction and drying equipment also works on a completely different scale than consumer tools. Truck-mounted extractors remove water hundreds of times faster than a shop vacuum, and commercial dehumidifiers pull 10 to 20 times more moisture from the air than residential units. That speed and power are essential when you are racing the 24 to 48 hour window before mold growth begins.

Warning Signs for Burst Pipe Repair

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • Ceilings or walls that show damp patches, stains or a swollen bulge
  • Brownish water at the faucet, which can mean corroded pipes
  • A hidden leak often shows up first as a much larger water bill
  • Stale or earthy smells coming from a cabinet or wall cavity
  • A spray or steady drip coming off pipework you can see
  • No water at a tap in freezing weather, a sign the line is frozen
  • The sound of running water when no fixtures are in use
